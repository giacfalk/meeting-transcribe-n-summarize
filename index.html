🔒

Private by design

Transcription runs locally with faster-whisper. No bot joins your call, no audio is uploaded, and nothing is stored on someone else's server. The only thing that can go online is the text sent to summarize it, and even that can run fully offline with a local model through Ollama.

Everything in one quiet little app

No plugins, no accounts, no bots in your meeting. Just record, and get a clean, speaker-labelled transcript and a summary.

🎙️

One button

Click Start Recording, then Stop. That's all you have to do.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Who said what

Your microphone and the system audio are recorded on separate channels, so every line is labelled Me or Others.

💻

Local transcription

faster-whisper runs on your machine, with automatic language detection and timestamps. The audio never leaves it.

📝

Automatic summaries

Markdown notes (overview, key points, decisions, action items) from Claude, the Anthropic or OpenAI (ChatGPT) API, or a fully local model.

🌍

Speaks your language

The interface is available in 15 languages, from Spanish and German to Chinese, Japanese and Hindi. Pick yours in Settings or the first-run wizard.

🔔

Offers to record

When Zoom, Teams or Meet start using the mic, a small prompt asks whether to record. It never records by itself.

⚡

Live transcript

Optionally watch a rough transcript build up as you talk, and get running notes on the fly, on by choice, off by default.

💾

Crash-safe

Audio is written to disk while you record, so a crash or power cut loses only the last couple of seconds.

🖥️

Works everywhere

Windows, macOS and Linux. A tray icon, a global hotkey, .srt subtitles and optional GPU transcription.

Frequently asked questions

Does Meeting Recorder upload my audio anywhere?

No. Transcription runs locally on your machine with faster-whisper, no audio is uploaded and no bot joins your call. Only the finished text can optionally be sent to summarize it, and even that can run fully offline with a local model.

Is Meeting Recorder free?

Yes, free for personal and any noncommercial use (research, education, non-profits, government). Commercial use needs a separate license.

Which platforms does it support?

Windows, macOS and Linux.

Does it work with Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet?

Yes. It records your microphone and the system audio together, so it captures any meeting app, no plugins and no bots.

Can it run completely offline?

Yes. Transcription is always local; for summaries you can use a fully local model through Ollama, so nothing ever goes online.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No account. Summaries can use your existing Claude login (no key needed), the Anthropic or OpenAI (ChatGPT) API, or a fully local model.

What languages is the app available in?

The interface comes in 15 languages: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Hindi. Transcription itself handles far more, with automatic language detection.
